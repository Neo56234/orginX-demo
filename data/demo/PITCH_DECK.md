## DESIGN NOTES FOR SLIDE CREATION

- **Background:** `#0A0E1A` (near-black)
- **Primary text:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Accent:** `#3D8BFF` (electric blue)
- **Alert/Mismatch:** `#FF3B5C` (red)
- **Authentic:** `#00D67E` (green)
- **Warning:** `#FFB833` (amber)
- **Font (English):** Inter or JetBrains Mono
- **Font (Bangla):** Noto Sans Bengali
- **Slide ratio:** 16:9
- **NO bullet points with decorative icons** — use clean text and data
- **ONE big idea per slide** — if you need more text, you're trying to say too much
